# Project vision — TravelOS AI

**TravelOS AI** — The Intelligent Operating System for Travel Agencies.

## Vision

Build an AI-powered SaaS platform that centralizes commercial, administrative, and customer-service operations for a travel agency.

We are not building “just a CRM”, “just a chatbot”, or “just a quoter”.  
We are building the most complete digital ecosystem for travel agencies in Latin America — the agency’s operations center.

## Problems (pain points)

1. Late responses lose clients.
2. Quotes take too long (manual PDFs, rates, itineraries, WhatsApp).
3. Information is scattered (Excel, WhatsApp, email, Drive, PDFs).
4. No follow-up / nurturing.
5. Knowledge walks out when advisors leave.
6. Repetitive work (docs, hours, requirements, links).
7. Opaque metrics (conversion, CAC, destinations, advisors).
8. Travelers expect mobile immediacy.

## MVP modules (pilot)

Smart CRM · AI sales agent (phased) · Intelligent quoter · Itinerary builder · Traveler portal · Automations · Management intelligence · Document basics · White-label tenancy.
